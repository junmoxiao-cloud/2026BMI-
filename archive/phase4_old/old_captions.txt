**English Caption**
Fig. 4. Multi-dimensional Feature Dependence and Generalization Robustness across Subject Groups.
(A-C) Relative performance retention curves under progressive perturbation across (A) Spectral, (B) Spatial, and (C) Temporal dimensions. The x-axis denotes the masking ratio (0.0 <= x <= 1.0), and the y-axis represents the normalized accuracy retention relative to the baseline. Solid lines indicate the mean degradation trajectory across all subject groups (N=15), fitted via cubic splines. Shaded regions denote the 95% confidence intervals (CI), illustrating the inter-subject variance and generalization stability. (D) Cross-dimensional robustness comparison. Overlay of the mean retention curves highlights the model's differential vulnerability.
Conclusion: The model exhibits catastrophic performance collapse under spatial perturbations (AUC = 0.22) while maintaining high robustness to temporal block shuffling (AUC = 0.88), revealing a strong bias toward spatial topology over temporal dynamics for feature decoding.
